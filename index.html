The concept

What's a liboké?

In Congolese cooking, a liboké is food gently steamed inside a banana leaf — sealed in, flavour intensified, nothing wasted. It's how we think about delivery too.

Authenticity, reinvented

We take beloved Congolese recipes and refine them for the table — familiar flavours, elevated presentation. This is home cooking treated like fine dining.

Delivery only, for now

No venue yet — just baby steps. We're starting small: a tight menu, made in small batches, delivered across Nairobi in limited monthly drops.

The bag

Your order, delivered in style.

Every Liboké House order arrives bundled with care in our signature canvas tote — sealed like a liboké, carried home like a gift.
